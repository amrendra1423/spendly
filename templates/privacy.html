Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense entries, categories, amounts, and dates you log while using the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in your dashboard. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third parties. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service, and these providers may process data on our behalf solely to support that infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
